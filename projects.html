Quadcopter restoration

I’m restoring an existing damaged quadcopter as a personal research project. I’ll inspect the airframe, identify its parts, and document repair and testing decisions as I go.

Current focus: Inspect the airframe and inventory its parts.

Goal and constraints

  • Goal: Restore the existing quadcopter and document the process.
  • Starting point: A damaged drone frame with parts to identify.
  • Resources: Personal research and a limited budget.
  • Budget: To be set after the parts inventory.
  • Size, payload, and flight time: To be determined after inspection.

Parts inventory

I’ll update this list as I identify each component.

  • Airframe: Identification in progress
  • Motors: To identify
  • Speed controllers: To identify
  • Flight controller: To identify
  • Battery and propellers: To inspect

Restoration plan

  1. Inspect the airframe and record visible damage.
  2. Identify the components and assess their condition.
  3. Decide what can be reused, repaired, or replaced.
  4. Document assembly and checks.
  5. Record test results and update the project requirements.
